1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Idle Businessman Tycoon (the "Game") handles information when you install and play it on an Android device. The Game is free to play and supported by advertising. By using the Game, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Game.

2. Who we are

The Game is published by an independent developer ("we", "us", "our"). The developer name and contact details are shown on the Game's Google Play Store listing.

3. Information the Game handles directly

This section covers data the Game itself creates or stores, as distinct from data collected by third-party services (Section 4).

3.1 Gameplay progress and settings

The Game may save your gameplay progress (for example, in-game currency, businesses and upgrades) and settings (for example, sound preferences) on your device so that you can continue playing. Information stored on your device may be affected by the backup and restore features you have enabled on your device.

3.2 Permissions

The Android permissions requested by the Game are declared in its app manifest and can be viewed in your device's app settings. Displaying ads through Google AdMob generally requires internet access, and may involve access to the Android advertising ID where the app declares that permission.

The advertising services described below may collect device-level information that can be considered personal data under some laws.

4. Information collected by third-party services

The Game uses the third-party services described below, which operate under their own privacy policies and may collect information independently of us.

4.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

The Game shows banner, interstitial (full-screen) and rewarded ads through Google AdMob, which uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google and its partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (for example, the Android Advertising ID), where available and not reset or disabled by you;
  • IP address, from which approximate location (such as country or city) may be derived;
  • Device and software information, such as device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen size, language, time zone, carrier and network type;
  • App information, such as the Game's package name and version;
  • Ad interaction data, such as ads requested, shown, clicked or completed (including rewarded ad completion), and related timestamps;
  • Diagnostics and performance data, such as crash logs, ad loading and SDK performance information; and
  • Cookie-like identifiers and local storage used by the SDK for ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping and fraud prevention.

This list is based on Google's general public description of AdMob and may change. For Google's own descriptions, see the links in Section 13. Google acts under its own policies for this data.

4.2 Google Play services

The Game runs on devices that use Google Play services, which may process certain device information as described in Google's policies. The advertising SDK also relies on Google Play services for some functions, such as retrieving the advertising ID.

4.3 Other services

This policy describes Google AdMob, the advertising service used by the Game. Other third-party components used to build or run the Game may also process technical information under their own policies. If we add services that collect user data, we intend to update this policy.

5. Personalized and non-personalized ads

Personalized ads are chosen using information such as your advertising ID and your past activity across apps and services, to show ads that may be more relevant to you. Non-personalized ads are chosen mainly using contextual information (for example, the content of the app and your approximate location) and do not use your advertising profile for tailoring, though data may still be collected for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.

Which type of ads you see can depend on your region, on your device and Google account settings, and on any ad choices or consent options made available to you.

You can also control ads on your device:

  • On Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path varies by device and Android version) to delete or reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization.
  • Manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

Opting out does not remove ads; it changes how they are selected.

We use information handled directly by the Game to operate the Game and to save your progress and settings. Third parties, such as Google, use the information they collect for the purposes described in their own policies, including delivering and measuring ads, preventing fraud and abuse, and maintaining and improving their services.

10. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. In some countries the minimum age for digital consent is higher than 13, and in those places the Game should only be used by people who meet that age or have a parent or guardian's permission. Advertising shown in the Game is subject to Google's own policies and settings.

If you believe a child under the applicable age is using the Game or has provided information through it, please contact us using the details in Section 15 so that we can look into it.
